## 👨‍💻 About Me

I'm the Founder of **[Finsensa](https://www.finsensa.com/)**, a UK fintech product being built to help SME finance advisers turn borrower information and financial documents into structured, lender-ready credit submissions.

My background spans more than a decade across **banking, SME and retail credit, portfolio management, healthcare finance, data analytics and consulting**.

I have worked across lending decisions, credit assessment, portfolio management, financial analysis and the operational problems that sit between businesses, advisers and lenders.

I later trained in full-stack software development to complement that domain experience with the ability to build products myself.

Today, my work sits at the intersection of:

- 💷 **SME finance and credit underwriting**
- 🏗️ **Fintech product development**
- 🤖 **AI-assisted financial analysis and workflow automation**
- 📊 **Data analytics and decision support**
- 💻 **Full-stack software development**

---

## 🚀 What I'm Building

I am currently building **Finsensa**, with a focus on helping commercial finance advisers reduce the manual work involved in reviewing borrower information, analysing financial documents and preparing lender-ready submissions.

The product is progressing towards pilot and early market validation with UK commercial finance professionals.

My work spans the underlying **credit logic, user workflows, product strategy and technical architecture**, alongside building and refining the product itself.

👉 **[Visit Finsensa](https://www.finsensa.com/)**

My background includes:

- SME and commercial credit
- Retail lending and credit products

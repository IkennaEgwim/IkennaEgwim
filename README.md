### What I'm building

**Finsensa helps UK commercial finance brokers know whether a business loan case stands up before the lender sees it.**

Most cases aren't declined because the business is bad. They're declined because the figures don't reconcile, the loan purpose doesn't match where the money is actually going, or the repayment story falls apart at the credit officer's first question. The broker usually finds out weeks later, after a decline or a long chain of emails.

Finsensa checks the case the way a lender would, while there's still time to fix it.

We're opening founding access to a small group of UK commercial finance brokers.
